B2B prospecting for freelancers

Break the feast-and-famine cycle, without spending your evenings on it.

Prospecting when you do everything yourself

A freelancer has no sales team. You deliver, you invoice, you handle admin — and prospecting comes last, when the pipeline runs dry. The classic pattern: two full months, one empty one, and prospecting restarted in a panic at the worst possible moment.

The problem is not motivation. It is that building a prospect list costs hours you cannot bill, and you always take those hours from time you do not have.

One hour a month, not a working day

You describe your typical client — a sector, an area. The engine identifies the matching companies, finds the decision maker's email, scores each prospect out of 10 and writes a personalised opener from their website.

You launch the sequence and follow-ups go out on their own at day+4, +9 and +14. They produce most of the replies, and they are exactly what gets forgotten when doing it by hand.

Regularity instead of urgency

Twenty companies contacted every week, without thinking about it, beats two hundred in a panic in January. It is the only known way to break the cycle. Credits never expire: a month without prospecting costs you nothing.

What is never charged

One credit equals one company scanned with a verified email. An address we cannot find costs you nothing and never enters your campaigns — which protects your sending domain's reputation, your most fragile asset when you have a single professional address.
