FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where does the data come from?

DataCloser queries Google Search and Google Maps in real time at the moment of your search, then analyzes each company's website. No resold databases, no stale data: every lead has a freshness date.

Is this GDPR compliant?

Yes. The collected data is public and professional (B2B). Email enrichment is done through a GDPR-certified provider, and every export states the source and collection date. Emails sent via Outreach automatically include an unsubscribe notice.

How does AI Outreach work?

Connect your mailbox (Gmail, Outlook, OVH…) via SMTP in the Outreach settings. Select a lead file, write your template with dynamic variables and launch. The AI drafts a personalized email for every lead using their icebreaker, checks coherence before sending, and sends from your own address with a randomized anti-spam delay. 1 credit = 1 sent email. Follow-ups (day +4, +9, +14) are free.

Are follow-ups really free?

Yes. The automatic follow-up on day +4/+9/+14 is free on all plans — no extra credit is charged. It's written by the AI in a different style from the first email, with a natural reminder tone.

How many leads do I get with my credits?

You only pay for usable leads. 1 credit is only deducted when a lead is delivered with a usable email (personally verified or likely catch-all) — along with the decision-maker, the /10 score and the icebreaker. Leads without a valid email, leads rejected for low score, or an interrupted scan are never charged. With 500 credits, the Starter plan covers intensive prospecting across multiple sectors and cities.

Which countries are covered?

Belgium, France, Luxembourg and Switzerland with native coverage, city by city. Manual input also lets you target any other country.

Can I connect DataCloser to my CRM?

Yes. Through the Exports & CRM tab you can send your leads to any CRM or automation tool via webhook (Make, Zapier, n8n…), alongside the classic CSV export.

Is there a minimum term?

None. Plans are monthly and cancellable anytime, and one-off credit packs without subscription start at €9.99.
